Privacy Policy & AI Pledge

Last Updated: October 2026 · Contact: sivanapuli01@yahoo.com

1. Our Sacred Privacy Commitment

At ugood, we believe that journaling is personal and sacred. Your reflections, raw feelings, and personal breakthroughs belong strictly to you. We do not sell your data, monetize your journal, or display advertisements.

2. Why and How We Use Generative AI

ugood uses Google Gemini exclusively as an empathetic reflection mirror—acting as your "Past Self" to cut through mental loops.

Zero-Model Training Guarantee Your personal journal entries, moods, and reflections are NEVER used to train, retrain, fine-tune, or improve any public or private artificial intelligence models.
  • Ephemeral Processing: Entry prompts sent to the Google Gemini API are processed in-flight solely to produce your brief 2–3 sentence lesson and overcome-reminder, and are immediately discarded.
  • No Commercial Profiling: We never share your writings with data brokers or behavioral trackers.

3. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

  • Account Details: When you register, your email address is authenticated and securely stored via Supabase Auth.
  • Journal Entries: Your written entries, dates, titles, and emotional states are encrypted and stored in your private Supabase database vault.

4. Not Medical or Crisis Advice

ugood is a mindfulness reflection tool. It does not provide psychiatric treatment, clinical therapy, medical diagnosis, or crisis intervention. If you are experiencing distress, please reach out to licensed mental health professionals or emergency resources.

5. Total Data Ownership & Deletion (GDPR Compliance)

  • Export Your Journey: You can export your complete journaling history to a clean Markdown archive or CSV file at any time directly in the app (Settings → Export Your Journey).
  • In-App Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated records with one click (Settings → Delete Account & Export All Data). This purges all your data from our database and automatically sends a CSV backup to your email.
