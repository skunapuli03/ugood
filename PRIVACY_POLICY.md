## 1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At **ugood**, we believe that journaling is sacred. Your personal reflections, vulnerabilities, and emotions belong to you and only you. We do not sell your data, monetize your personal thoughts, or display advertisements. 

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is stored, and transparently details **how and why Generative AI is used** within the app.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Account Information
When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials via **Supabase Auth**. This allows you to securely access your journal across your devices.

### B. Journal Entries & Reflection Data
We store the content you write, entry timestamps, titles, and emotional tags you select (e.g., Happy, Anxious, Content, Reflective). All entries are stored in encrypted cloud storage hosted on **Supabase** and are accessible only when authenticated with your account.

---

## 3. How and Why We Use Generative AI

### A. Purpose of Generative AI
ugood utilizes Google Gemini to act as a **"Past Self Reflection Companion."**  
When you write an entry, the AI reads your thoughts and past overcome challenges to produce:
1. **Concise Lessons (2–3 sentences)**: Grounded, specific reflections tied directly to your dilemma to help cut through mental noise.
2. **Resilience Insights**: Reminding you of past instances where you overcame similar anxieties or emotions.

### B. Zero-Model Training Pledge (Our Promise)
* **Never Used for Training**: Your private journal entries, thoughts, and moods are **NEVER** used to train, retrain, fine-tune, or improve any public or private artificial intelligence models.
* **Ephemeral Processing**: Requests sent to the Google Gemini API are processed in-flight. Prompts and completions are not retained to train baseline models.
* **No Third-Party Ad Profiling**: We do not share your entry text with data brokers, advertisers, or behavioral trackers.

## 4. Not Medical or Mental Health Advice

ugood is designed as a personal self-reflection and mindfulness tool. **ugood and its AI features do not provide medical diagnosis, therapy, psychiatric treatment, or clinical crisis counseling.** If you are experiencing a mental health crisis or emergency, please contact professional emergency services or a licensed healthcare provider immediately.

---

## 5. Data Ownership, Export, and Deletion (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

We believe you should have total sovereignty over your data:

* **Export Your Journey**: You can export your entire journaling history into a clean Markdown archive or CSV file at any time directly in the app (**Settings → Export Your Journey**).
* **Account Deletion**: You can permanently delete your account directly inside the app (**Settings → Delete Account & Export All Data**). Upon confirmation:
  1. A complete CSV archive of all your entries is generated and prepared for your personal records.
  2. All your entries, account records, and generated insights are permanently purged from our Supabase database.
